1. Project Title / Headline

   
# Blinkit-Dashboard

​Blinkit Performance Insights: India’s Last Minute App Sales Dashboard
​A comprehensive data visualization tool designed to analyze key performance indicators for Blinkit, focusing on sales trends, outlet performance, item attributes, and regional impact.Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Blinkit sales performance, outlet establishment trends, product categories, outlet sizes, customer ratings, and key business KPIs. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making through dynamic filters and visual analytics.


​2. Short Description / Purpose
​The Blinkit Performance Dashboard is a visually engaging Power BI report designed to provide actionable insights into outlet establishment, sales performance, and item-level data. It allows stakeholders to analyze key metrics like total sales, average ratings, and item distribution across various outlet sizes, locations, and product types to optimize operational strategies and drive business growth.


​3. Tech Stack
​The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

​* Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation and interactivity.
​* Data Modeling – Relationships established to connect outlet data, sales figures, and item attributes for dynamic cross-filtering and accurate aggregation.
​* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculating total sales, average sales, and aggregate ratings across different dimensions.
​* Visualizations – Utilization of various chart types (KPI cards, area charts, donut charts, bar charts, and tables) to represent complex business metrics intuitively.


4. Strategic Business Impact
    
​* Operational Optimization: Tracking metrics like "Total Sales" and "Average Sales" enables the business to monitor financial health in real-time. The "Outlet Establishment" trend line helps leadership evaluate growth patterns and seasonal peaks, facilitating better resource allocation across the network.  
​* Customer Engagement & Strategy: Insights into "Item Type" and "Fat Content" performance allow the company to tailor inventory strategies to evolving consumption patterns, such as the demand for healthier, low-fat options.  
​* Performance Benchmarking: The dashboard allows for an objective comparison of different "Outlet Types" and "Outlet Locations," which helps identify underperforming stores that may require targeted marketing or operational adjustments.  
​* Revenue Protection: By monitoring average ratings and sales data, stakeholders can pinpoint product categories or outlets that drive customer retention, allowing the platform to prioritize high-demand items that disproportionately contribute to revenue.  


​5. Key Insights Derived from the Dashboard 


​* Regional Performance: The "Outlet Location" visualization (Tier 1–3) helps stakeholders understand how geographic distribution impacts total sales, enabling agile adjustments to supply chain and marketing tactics based on regional demand.  
​* Product Assortment: Categorizing sales by "Item Type" highlights which categories  act as key drivers of customer frequency, which is essential for maintaining inventory turnover and filling rates.
